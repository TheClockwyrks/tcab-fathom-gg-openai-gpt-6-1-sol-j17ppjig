# Fathom

One small light. An ocean of things that can see it. Slip into a flooded stone maze
as a glowing forager, grazing pinpricks of plankton while the trench wakes around
you. Eat to widen your window into the dark—but remember that a brighter meal
makes a brighter target.

The corridors hold three very different hunters. An amber glimmer might be a
harmless drifter, or the Lanternjaw wearing its disguise. The eyeless Gloamfin
listens for you, driving hard along straight passages and losing its edge at
corners. Somewhere beyond your light, a Flarefish charges a sudden bloom that can
turn an unseen room into a chase.

Send sonar around a bend to trace the route ahead and catch a violet silhouette.
Just know that your pulse carries both ways: the listener now knows where you
are. Leave ink behind to break a light hunter's fix, double back through the wrap
tunnel, or stop in the quiet and let your glow fade. Remembered paths remain, but
only inside the circle you carry.

Clear the last mote and descend. Your score and remaining lives come with you;
the new maze starts dark, with more hunters and a shorter reach for your sonar.
Every dive asks the same question: how much light can you afford?
